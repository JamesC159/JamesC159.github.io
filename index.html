01 — About

Three tools for success.

“I believe success can come with three tools. Valuing knowledge, continuous innovative thinking, and believing in yourself and your peers.”

My career started close to the metal — mainframe security features on ClearPath OS 2200, FIPS 140-2 cryptographic validation, and micro-segmented network security on IoT edge devices at Unisys. That foundation shapes how I build for the web today: security-minded, pipeline-first, and allergic to toil.

Since then I've delivered customer-facing serverless microservices for web and mobile at Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota, built Next.js and AWS AppSync products at CaringBridge, and now help drive an organization-wide Backstage implementation at Thrivent. Along the way I've mentored interns, interviewed engineers, and carried the pager.
